How to review
  1. Read the full excerpt. Use only the information it contains; keep uncertain or noisy wording uncertain.
  2. For each question, select A–D and copy a short supporting quotation. For an absence claim, explain what the excerpt does and does not establish.
  3. If no single option fits, select Cannot choose one option and explain why. You can also select a best answer and flag it for discussion.
  4. Export your answers before closing or switching devices. After at least two people finish the same items independently, compare answers and resolve disagreements. Keep both original files.

Interpretation rules

A suggestion proposes an action. An intention expresses a plan. A commitment accepts responsibility. A conditional commitment applies only under its stated conditions.

A request or authorization alone does not establish personal acceptance. Keep a reported obligation distinct from a promise made by the person themselves.

Changes and completion

Preserve conditions until explicitly revised. A requested reassignment needs acceptance or handover evidence. Satisfying a condition does not prove the task is complete.

Use the latest supported status and retain earlier dates when asked. A completion report establishes what was reported, not independent verification.

Transcripts and answer choices are unchanged from the experiment. Real meeting excerpts contain transcription errors and may lack surrounding context.

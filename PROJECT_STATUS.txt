PROJECT RECAP
The goal is to remember who has agreed to do what as a conversation changes. A summary can preserve the general topic while losing a condition, owner, cancellation, or deadline revision. We test whether an explicit evidence-linked ledger helps, using the same model to answer identical questions from different representations.

MIDTERM STOPPING POINT
A documented small dataset, a runnable tracker and baselines, preliminary measurements, inspectable errors, and an updated plan. We do not need a finished benchmark, fine-tuning, forecasting, or a novel algorithm for the midterm.

WHAT IS IMPLEMENTED
- A draft annotation guide separating suggestions, intentions, acceptance, conditions, and completion.
- Twelve public MeetingBank segments and ten synthetic conversations, with 66 draft QA probes.
- Development/pilot splits grouped by source meeting.
- Transcript, reference-summary, generated-summary, and incremental-tracker conditions.
- A question-only control to expose shortcut answers.
- Qwen3.8-27B inference in Colab on A100 40GB using 4-bit weights.
- Exact evidence checks, saved state history, raw model responses, caching, and analysis scripts.

HUMAN REVIEW NEEDED BEFORE SUBMISSION
1. Two team members independently use review/review.html and export separate answer files, or use review_blind.txt with independent_review_template.json. Do not consult review_key_draft.txt first.
2. Run review_agreement.py on the two completed files; adjudicate disagreements and preserve the originals. There are 66 questions total, including 48 pilot questions across 16 conversations.
3. Inspect the error examples and ambiguous labels. Save adjudicated labels under a new filename and rescore against them; retain the original AI draft for transparency.
4. Add the team members' actual contributions and verify the AI-assistance disclosure in the report. Do not claim independent review until it occurs.
5. Confirm the instructor's required formatting and submission destination. This package does not submit anything.

ONE-DAY ALLOCATION
Setup, guide, sampling, and code: approximately 2-3 hours.
GPU pilot plus parallel human annotation: approximately 2-3 hours, dependent on inference speed.
Error review, scoring, and report revision: approximately 1-2 hours.
Final packaging and team verification: approximately 1 hour.
Use the remaining days before the communicated deadline as a buffer for annotation disagreements or requested revisions.
